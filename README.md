## ⚡ Executive Summary

Based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**, I am the Founder of **GrayVally**, architecting scalable digital systems where **engineering precision** meets **conversion-driven design**.

- 🔭 **Founder Mindset:** Building a suite of SaaS products including **CardCraft Pro** and the **GrayVally Ecosystem**
- 💻 **Full-Stack Craft:** React & Next.js specialist with Node.js and cloud-native architecture
- 🎨 **Design Authority:** Interfaces optimized for performance and conversion (Lighthouse 95+)

Trusted by startups and international clients across SaaS, e-commerce, and enterprise platforms.
